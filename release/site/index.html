Make the signals yours.

Choose a color, brightness, and steady or slow-flashing light for each state. Completion stays on for 5 seconds by default, adjustable from 1–60 seconds.

Task signals stay local.

No Keyphore account or automatic telemetry uploads. Only necessary task-event fields are handled—not prompts, conversations, or tool content.

Requires Codex desktop or CLI and one keyboard with stock firmware connected over USB. See the catalog above for model verification status. Bluetooth and 2.4 GHz connections are not supported.
