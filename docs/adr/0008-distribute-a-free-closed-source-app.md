# Distribute a free closed-source App

Superseded on 2026-09-04: Keyphore now uses GPL-3.0-only for its first-party code, including the Swift App and Companion. GPL distributions must provide the corresponding source under the license terms. Repository visibility is a separate publication decision. The original decision below is retained as history.

The compiled Keyphore App will be available to users at no charge through a public product page, notarized DMG, and signed update feed while its first-party Swift source and repository remain private, choosing product control over public source auditability. Existing third-party license and attribution obligations, including the embedded NuPhyIO protocol notice, remain in force; the current repository's MIT-marked Rust history does not make the rewritten Swift App or its private repository open source.
