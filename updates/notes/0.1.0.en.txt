Keyphore 0.1.0

Show Codex task status on the NuPhy Air65 V3 main backlight. Supports Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later and a wired USB connection.

This release fixes interrupted tasks remaining active, late Hook events reviving completed tasks, managed removal of Codex configuration, quit during setup, and unavailable keyboard health reporting. It reduces background setup polling and refines the About page, Quit button, and panel icon.

The App and DMG are Developer ID signed and Apple notarized.
